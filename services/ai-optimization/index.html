02 / HOW AI READS CONTENT

AI extracts. It does not browse.

AI systems don't read the way humans do. They extract, analyze, and synthesize. Content buried in paragraphs, vague in its claims, or unstructured for machine parsing simply gets skipped.

WHAT WE RESTRUCTURE
  • Answers surfaced to the top — clear and citable
  • Schema and structured data: FAQ, LocalBusiness, Service markup
  • Entity authority built across trusted platforms
  • Content optimized per platform preference
PLATFORM DIFFERENCES
  • ChatGPT favors depth and demonstrated authority
  • Perplexity favors fresh, well-cited, sourced content
  • Google AI Overviews favor top-ten organic results
  • Each requires a different content signal — we handle all three
