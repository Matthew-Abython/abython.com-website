Content Structure for AI Extraction

AI systems don't read like humans.
They extract, analyze, and synthesize.

What we do:

  1. Restructure your content — answer questions at the top (not buried in paragraphs) so AI extracts clear, citable statements
  2. Add schema & structured data — FAQ markup, LocalBusiness schema, Service schema; helps machines understand what you offer
  3. Build entity authority — ensure your business is mentioned in the right context, link to relevant topics, get cited on trusted platforms
  4. Optimize for each platform — ChatGPT prefers authoritative, in-depth content; Perplexity favors fresh, well-cited articles; Google AI Overviews favor content in top-10 organic results

The result: your content gets cited in AI answers.
Users see your name. They trust it. They call you.
