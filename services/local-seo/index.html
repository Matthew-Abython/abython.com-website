Your Most Powerful Asset

Google Business Profile carries 32% of local ranking weight. It is, quite literally, the most important thing we optimize — and most practices have left it incomplete.

What we do:

  • Audit every field — most GBPs are less than 40% complete
  • Fill in categories, service areas, hours, photos, videos, and descriptions
  • Build a review collection system and establish a consistent posting schedule
  • Populate the Q&A section with real patient questions

A complete GBP earns 7× more clicks than an incomplete one. This phase alone produces visible results within two to four weeks.

Reviews That Rank and Convert

Reviews aren't just reputation — they directly affect your visibility. Google weighs review quantity, recency, and consistency. A practice with a steady flow of fresh reviews consistently outranks one with more reviews that stopped six months ago.

What we handle:

  • A system to collect genuine reviews via text and email at the right moment
  • Responses to every review — positive and critical — in your voice
  • Monitoring across Google, Yelp, and Healthgrades
  • Review velocity tracking to ensure the signal stays consistent

Active, Not Set-and-Forget

Local search is not a one-time setup. Google rewards practices that stay active — and punishes those that go quiet. Most of your competitors check their GBP once a year. The ones winning check weekly.

What we monitor every month:

  • Local ranking changes and competitor activity
  • Citation consistency and directory updates
