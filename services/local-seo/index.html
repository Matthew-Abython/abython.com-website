Your GBP Is Your Most Powerful Asset

Google Business Profile now carries 32% of local ranking weight.
It's literally the most important thing we optimize.

What we do:

  • Audit every field (most GBPs are 40% incomplete)
  • Fill in: categories, service areas, business hours (including holidays), photos, videos, descriptions
  • Set up review collection system and create a posting schedule
  • Build Q&A section with real customer questions

A complete GBP gets 7× more clicks than an incomplete one.
This alone drives visible results in 2–4 weeks.

Reviews Influence Rankings and Decisions

Reviews aren't just about reputation. They directly affect visibility.
Google weights review quantity, recency, and consistency.

What we handle:

  • Build a system to collect genuine reviews (texts, emails, post-job follow-up)
  • Respond to every review (positive and negative)
  • Monitor across Google, Yelp, Facebook
  • Track review velocity (consistent flow signals active business)

A business with fresh, consistent reviews outranks competitors with old, stagnant ones—even if they're technically more complete.
