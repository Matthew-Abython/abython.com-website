About the Chef

Grace has cooked in three Michelin kitchens.

After training under two Michelin-starred chefs in Lyon and staging in Copenhagen, Grace returned to Illinois to do something quieter — and more personal. She founded Graces Private Food Services in 2021 to bring serious cooking to dinner tables, not restaurant counters.

"Every client is one client. Your table gets my full attention — not a team running twelve covers at once."

— Grace, Chef & Founder

How It Works

From first call to last bite — we handle everything.

01

Consult

We start with a 20-minute call — your household size, preferences, restrictions, and goals. No form, just a conversation.

02

Menu Design

Grace designs a seasonal menu tailored to you, sourcing locally where possible and adjusting based on your feedback before a single ingredient is purchased.

03

Shopping & Prep

We shop, prep, cook, and plate — either in your kitchen or ours. Everything is labeled, dated, and stored properly before we leave.

04

Enjoy

Heat, plate, and eat. We provide simple reheating instructions for every dish and are always reachable if you have questions.
